Automated win-back offers

Sent automatically once a day by winback-processor: 50% off 1 month to free accounts inactive 30+ days (never upgraded), and 50% off 6 months 5 days after someone cancels Premium. Each account can only ever receive a given offer type once — see migration 0030's unique constraint. Redemption itself isn't wired up yet (no real billing to discount against), so "Redeemed" will stay empty until that lands.

Hero Helpers & Owners

Owner gets the same plus full admin control, including this dashboard built into the app. Every grant needs a confirmation code sent to your own email before it takes effect — revoking doesn't need one.
